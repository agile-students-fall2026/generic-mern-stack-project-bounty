# Contributing

## Team Norms
The team communicates via Discord in the Knowledge Kitchen server under the Agile Dev section in the team channels, specifically "bounty". We have frequent messages and some private discussions/calls regarding ideas, help needed, and conflicts. Responses are expected by the next assignment due date. When failing in obligations, the professor will be contacted. 

Our sprints take approximately 2 weeks. We will have standups at least thrice a week, with at least 2 during the weekdays for a few minutes. Members are expected to, at the very least, be able to attend via video call if not in-person (and under difficult circumstances communicate as such to both the team and the professor). Members who make no progress will be reported to the professor. 

The team uses the [feature branch version control workflow](https://knowledge.kitchen/content/courses/agile-development-and-devops/notes/feature-branch-workflow/). 

## Rules for Contributing
This is a project for a university class, so it is not expected that people contribute until December (or even afterwards).
